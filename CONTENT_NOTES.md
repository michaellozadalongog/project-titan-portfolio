# Project TITAN Content Notes

- Component count is presented as **9 documented components: 7 custom + 2 commercial bearings**.
- FEA results are labeled as **Fusion-reported simulation outputs** rather than physical validation.
- Design intent is 6061-T6 aluminum, but the material-property audit remains open because the reported stress and safety factor imply an effective yield value near 130.6 MPa.
- Prototype fabrication, physical testing, fatigue analysis, human factors, and clinical evaluation are clearly listed as future work.
- No claim is made that this project complies with FDA, ISO 13485, ISO 14971, or other regulated development requirements.

Mechanical Design & Engineering Validation

01 · Engineering Overview

Designed as a complete engineering workflow—not only a CAD model.

Project TITAN demonstrates the traceable process used to move from design intent to analytical evidence and manufacturing documentation. The project remains explicitly educational while applying professional engineering habits.

01

Problem & Scope

Develop a modular mechanical architecture representing the structural core of a wearable knee-support concept while documenting assumptions, constraints, and future validation needs.

02

Mechanical Architecture

Side plates, upper and lower frames, central shaft, commercial bearings, spacers, and mechanical hard-stop features organized into a serviceable assembly.

03

Verification Evidence

Static FEA, safety-factor assessment, displacement review, engineering drawings, material trade study, educational FMEA, and requirements traceability.

02 · Development Workflow

Requirements → CAD → Analysis → Documentation

01

Requirements

Defined scope, functional intent, structural targets, assumptions, and acceptance criteria.

02

Parametric CAD

Created custom components, assembly relationships, bearing interfaces, and interference-free geometry.

03

Simulation

Applied two 2,000 N bearing loads, refined the mesh, resolved unconstrained groups, and reviewed stress and displacement.

04

Drawings

Produced individual component drawings with dimensions, material notes, tolerances, quantity, and revision identification.

05

Design Controls Awareness

Added traceability, risk analysis, material trade study, decision log, manufacturing plan, and prototype plan.

03 · Engineering Validation

Structural performance under the defined static load case.

The final study applied two bearing loads of 2,000 N each to the inner cylindrical bearing surfaces. The reported values below are specific to the modeled materials, contacts, constraints, mesh, and static assumptions.

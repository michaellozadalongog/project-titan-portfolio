Engineering workflow

More than a finished model.

The project is organized as a traceable sequence from design inputs to verification evidence and future prototype planning.

  1. 01

    Requirements

    Defined project scope, design targets, assumptions, and verification methods.

  2. 02

    Architecture

    Mapped structural components, interfaces, load paths, and assembly hierarchy.

  3. 03

    Parametric CAD

    Created modular components and constrained the assembly in Fusion 360.

  4. 04

    Static FEA

    Evaluated stress, displacement, constraints, contacts, and safety factor.

  5. 05

    Documentation

    Produced drawings, requirements traceability, FMEA, and manufacturing plans.

  6. 06

    Future validation

    Defined physical prototyping and testing as the next project phase.

Flagship case study

A wearable knee-support mechanism explored as an educational mechanical system.

The assembly uses two structural side plates, upper and lower frame members, a central shaft, two spacers, and two commercial bearings. The work demonstrates design intent, simulation literacy, drawing creation, and engineering documentation without claiming clinical readiness.

  • Seven custom-designed structural components
  • Two commercial bearing components
  • Two 2,000 N simulated bearing loads
  • Ten-document engineering suite

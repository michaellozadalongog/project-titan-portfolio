Technical Data Package contents

What is included.

  • Ten controlled engineering documents in PDF and DOCX
  • Project TITAN Engineering Design Report
  • Load and geometry measurement evidence
  • Configuration and revision records
  • Requirements, risk, manufacturing, and prototype planning

Documentation boundary

This suite demonstrates educational engineering practice. It is not a Design History File, regulatory submission, clinical validation package, or evidence of ISO/FDA compliance.

Physical prototype fabrication, dimensional inspection, fatigue testing, human factors evaluation, and experimental validation remain future work.
